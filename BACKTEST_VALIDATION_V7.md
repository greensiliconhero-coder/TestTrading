# Backtest validation v7

Freeze before running: a cash/positions ledger with retained positions, net quantity adjustments, transaction fees, whole shares, configurable sale-proceeds settlement delay, verified split/bonus entitlement handling and dividend receivables paid only on the provided payment session. Trades require an earlier signal timestamp. Reject adjusted price basis, unsupported rights/demergers, ambiguous fractional entitlements, missing verification evidence and missing prices. Such rejection does not solve missing market data; it prevents silent use.

Use deterministic accounting cases with hand-computable equity and conservation checks. Preserve v6 output; do not overwrite or present new market returns. Ledger targets are externally supplied quantities, not an alpha strategy or a broker order system. A configurable one-session settlement delay is a test assumption, not a certified historical settlement calendar.

Synthetic learning controls: 20 fixed seeds, each 6,000 observations, chronological 3,600/1,200/1,200 splits; three causal input features. Positive control: future return = 0.01 times the first known feature plus Gaussian noise (sigma .005). Null: independent noise. Fit unchanged Ridge(alpha=100) and histogram tree settings. Choose one of gross forecast thresholds 0/.002/.004 on validation, only if mean net return after 20bps is positive; otherwise cash. Freeze all choices before accessing each synthetic test period. Preserve fitted models, exact arrays, predictions, selections and per-seed outcomes. Do not retry seeds or tune settings to force acceptance.

Acceptance gates: Ridge average planted-signal test correlation >= .7; absolute signed mean null test correlation <= .05 for each learner; at most 30% of null runs may produce both a validation-selected rule and positive realized test mean. These are engineering smoke-test tolerances, not p-values or universal statistical guarantees. One failed seeded null case need not imply a defect; report the full distribution. Verify feature construction does not change when future data changes; purge labels overlapping a split boundary.

Market-data gate remains separate: verify price basis, full adjustment history through the vendor snapshot, verified factors/ex-dates/payment dates, source evidence and historical fee/settlement assumptions before an executable historical performance claim. Announcement timestamps are required when trading decisions depend on advance action knowledge; they are not the same as ex-date accounting inputs.
